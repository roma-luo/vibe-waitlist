Privacy policy · Updated October 2026

What happens to your information

vibe is not live yet. This page covers the waitlist only.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Your answers to the optional questions (your city and what you want most from vibe), if you give them.
  • How you found us: the ad or link you came from, the headline you saw, the page that sent you, and whether you used a phone or a computer.
  • Anonymous page activity, such as whether you scrolled or tapped a button, tied to a random ID stored in your browser.

Why we collect it

  • To email you when vibe launches.
  • To understand which messages and channels bring people to vibe, so we can decide where to launch and what to build.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the services that store it and send our emails.

Your choices

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. To have your email removed from the waitlist entirely, reply to any vibe email and ask.
