Naming rules

  • Lowercase, no spaces. Use _ between words. The page lowercases and strips anything else.
  • Never put a name or other identifying detail in a link value.
  • Write each link and its date in your tracking notes so you can match numbers to posts later.
  • For your own visits, open the page once with ?internal=1. That browser is then excluded from the Summary. ?internal=0 turns it off.
