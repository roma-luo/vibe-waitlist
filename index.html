Questions

When does vibe launch?

We're building it now, starting with small dinners in New York City. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the moment it opens.

Which cities?

New York City first. After you join, you can tell us which city you're in. That helps us decide where to go next.

Is it free?

Joining the waitlist is free. We'll share pricing before launch.

Is this a dating app?

It's for meeting people you click with. Dinners are small groups of four, so there's none of the pressure of a one-on-one first date.

How do photos and privacy work?

There's no wall of profile photos. You decide whether to see other guests' photos before a dinner. Feedback like "I like their vibe" is private, and the other person never sees it.

What do you do with my email?

We use it to tell you about vibe's launch. We don't sell it, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Details are in our privacy policy.
